# Pacta: 25-second intro video

**Format:** 16:9 at 1080p (export a 9:16 cut for social media). 25 seconds, 5 shots of about 5 seconds each.
**Look:** warm, flat 2D motion graphics. Cream background (#F7F4EE), deep green brand color (#1E6B47), dark ink lines (#1D1913), amber highlights (#C98A0B). Rounded, friendly shapes and smooth eased transitions. Small geometric stripe accents inspired by the Bolivian aguayo textile.
**Music:** upbeat, warm acoustic with a light charango melody and soft percussion. It is tense in shot 2, lifts in shot 3 and ends on a short logo sting.

> **Tip:** AI video tools render text and logos badly. Generate the shots without words, then add the on-screen text, the Pacta logo and the official Stellar logo in your editor (CapCut, Canva or Premiere).

> **Built version:** [video/](../video/README.md) renders this video in code (16:9 and 9:16, ES and EN), with no AI video tool. The voiceover below takes about 22 s to say, so each shot runs as long as its line and the cut is about 29 s. The 25 s timing in the table is the original plan.

## Master prompt (for tools that take a single prompt)

```
25-second explainer animation, flat 2D motion graphics, warm cream background, deep green and amber palette, rounded friendly shapes, smooth eased transitions, subtle Andean geometric stripe accents. Shot 1: a colorful Bolivian market stall with milk bottles, eggs and vegetables; the price tags flip upward faster and faster while a coin shrinks. Shot 2: a hand gives coins to a seller in exchange for a paper ticket for a future product; the seller and the coins vanish in a puff of smoke, leaving the buyer holding an empty ticket. Shot 3: rewind; the coins instead fly into a glowing green padlock vault floating in the center, encircled by a soft orbit ring, while a farmer's cow and field grow on the left. Shot 4: a buyer at a doorstep scans a QR code on a phone, the vault opens and the coins flow to the smiling farmer, and gold stars stack up above the farmer; a quick split shows an alternate path where the coins fly back to the buyer. Shot 5: the products morph from milk to eggs to a vegetable basket to a modern apartment building, then collapse into a clean empty center space for a logo. Optimistic, trustworthy, modern fintech mood. No text, no letters, no real brand logos, no realistic faces, no neon, no crypto coins with symbols.
```
